Easy Setup

How to Sideload LANStream TV on Your Television

Choose whichever method is most convenient for your home setup:

1

USB Flash Drive (Easiest)

Copy LANStream-TV.apk to a FAT32 USB drive. Plug it into your TV, open any TV File Manager (e.g. File Commander from Google Play), and click to install.

2

Send Files to TV App

Install the free Send Files to TV (SFTV) app from Google Play on both your phone and TV. Download the APK on your phone and beam it wirelessly to your TV.

3

Wireless ADB

Connect your computer via Wi-Fi ADB and install in one command:
adb connect <tv-ip>:5555
adb install -r LANStream-TV.apk

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions & Answers

Does LANStream require an active internet connection?

No. LANStream operates completely within your local network. You can connect your phone and TV to your home Wi-Fi router without internet, or turn on your phone's personal hotspot and connect your TV directly to the phone with zero cellular data consumption.

Why should I use LANStream instead of Google Cast or Miracast?

Miracast and screen casting mirror your phone's screen by re-encoding frames as a compressed video stream. This wastes battery, caps resolutions at 1080p, causes dropped frames, and prevents you from using your phone while playing. LANStream serves raw media bitstreams directly over HTTP/FTP; the TV decodes the video natively at full 4K HDR quality with 5.1/7.1 audio while your phone sleeps.

Can I access the server from my PC or laptop browser?

Yes! The Ktor HTTP engine includes an integrated responsive web interface. Simply navigate to http://<phone-ip>:8080/ on any desktop web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari) on the same Wi-Fi network to browse directories, stream movies, and upload files directly to your phone.

How do I report bugs or device crashes?

As this is our initial v1.0.0 release, please report any bugs directly on our GitHub Issues tracker with your device model, Android OS version, and steps to reproduce.
