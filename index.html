Easy Setup

How to Install LANStream TV on Your Television

Choose whichever method is easiest for you:

1

USB Pen Drive (Easiest)

Copy LANStream-TV.apk to a USB pen drive. Plug it into your TV, open any TV File Manager (such as File Commander from Google Play), and tap to install.

2

Send Files to TV App

Install the free Send Files to TV app from Google Play on both your phone and TV. Download the TV APK on your phone and beam it wirelessly to your TV.

3

Computer / Wireless ADB

For tech users, install directly from your computer over Wi-Fi:
adb connect <tv-ip>:5555
adb install -r LANStream-TV.apk

Behind the Project

The Story Behind LANStream

“Tired of screen-mirroring lag, reduced picture quality, and burning phone battery, I built LANStream to give you a true cinema experience at home.”

Most casting tools simply mirror your phone's screen—which compresses the video, introduces annoying lag, and drains your phone battery while forcing your screen to stay on. LANStream works differently: it streams the original, full-quality 4K video directly to your television over your local home Wi-Fi. Your TV decodes the video smoothly, and your phone can stay locked and resting quietly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful Answers to Common Questions

Does LANStream require an internet connection?

No internet is required! LANStream runs completely inside your local home network. You can connect your phone and TV to your home Wi-Fi router (even with no internet access), or turn on your phone's hotspot and connect your TV directly to the phone with zero cellular data used.

Why is LANStream better than regular screen mirroring?

Screen mirroring simply copies your phone's screen onto the TV by re-compressing it. This causes lag, limits resolution to 1080p, heats up your phone, and drains battery because your phone screen has to stay on. LANStream sends the actual video file directly to your TV. Your TV plays it in crystal-clear 4K HDR quality while your phone screen turns off and rests.

Can I also play videos on my computer or laptop?

Yes! When LANStream Mobile is running on your phone, open any web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge) on your PC or Mac and enter the link shown on your phone. You can watch videos, browse folders, and even upload new files straight from your computer.

How do I report an issue or suggest a feature?

You can report issues or share feedback anytime on our GitHub Issues page. Just describe your device model and what happened, and we'll look into it right away.
